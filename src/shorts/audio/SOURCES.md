- **drum_sting**: "Timpani Sting" — short dramatic timpani hit.
- **music**: "Happy Commercial Music" — bright, cheerful, playful
  acoustic-guitar-based loop (author's tags: bright, cheerful, feelgood,
  groovy, happy, playful), instrumental, no vocals. Shipped excerpt is the
  steady-state section after the quiet intro, suitable for looping/trimming.
